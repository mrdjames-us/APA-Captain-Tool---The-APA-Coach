Data Deletion Instructions — APA Captain

You can remove specific data yourself at any time while signed in to the app:

  • Disconnect poolplayers.com — go to the APA Sync tab and click Disconnect. This immediately deletes the stored sync token; nothing further is retained from poolplayers.com.
  • Delete players, schedule entries, or match history — use the delete controls on the Roster, Schedule, and Stats & History tabs.

Full account deletion

To delete your account entirely — including your sign-in record and all roster, schedule, and match data associated with it — email david.james@jamesnetworks.net from the address associated with your account and request deletion. We will confirm once your data has been removed, typically within a few days.
