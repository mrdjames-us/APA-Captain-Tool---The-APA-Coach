Privacy Policy — APA Captain

Last updated: July 2026

APA Captain ("the app") is a personal team-management tool for APA (American Poolplayers Association) team captains. This page explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Account sign-in. When you sign in with Google or Facebook, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from that provider, via Firebase Authentication.
  • poolplayers.com (APA) sync. If you choose to connect your APA account, your email and password are sent once to poolplayers.com to authenticate and are never stored by us. We store only a revocable device token that lets the app re-sync your roster and schedule without asking for your password again. You can disconnect at any time from the APA Sync tab, which deletes this token.
  • Team data. Roster, schedule, and match information you enter or sync from poolplayers.com is stored under your account so the app can display it back to you.

How your information is used

Your data is used solely to operate the app for you — displaying your roster, schedule, and match history, and helping you plan lineups. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with third parties except the infrastructure providers below, who process it on our behalf.

Where your data is stored

Data is stored in Google Firebase / Cloud Firestore, restricted so that only you can read or write your own data. The app itself is hosted on Cloudflare.

Data deletion

See our Data Deletion Instructions page for how to request deletion of your account and data.
